# Style lock — Yugi Jo Strategy Deck

Project: `~/.hermes/skills/nova-youtube-agent/deck/` (published to `Yugi-Jo/yugi-jo-strategy-deck`).
Created 2026-11-20 alongside the `graphic-design` skill's first real build.

## Direction contract

- **Surface**: a presented strategy report. Read on screen, often projected. Not an app, not a marketing page.
- **Audience**: Joel, deciding what to film next. Secondary: anyone he shows the numbers to.
- **Visitor mode**: skim for the finding, then read the detail if it earns it.
- **Visual lane**: premium / confident. Data-led, restrained, editorial. Confident without tipping playful.
- **Dials**: variance low, motion moderate, density medium, art direction restrained.
- **Thesis**: the data should be the imagery. In a report, charts and numbers carry the argument and text is the caption.
- **Risk**: a dark data deck can drift into dashboard cosplay. Countered by keeping type large, spacing generous, and every chart honest to its scale.

## Color contract (mood: premium, mode: dark)

Generated by `generate_palette.py --mood premium --mode dark`, then verified with
`check_contrast.py --matrix`. Locked palette:

| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| text | `#eff1fe` |
| bg | `#0a0a10` |
| surface | `#14151b` |
| primary | `#6368d5` |
| on-primary | `#ffffff` |
| secondary | `#272a4a` |
| accent | `#f1744d` |
| border | `#202127` |

**Legal pairings (from the matrix, do not invent new ones):**

- **Text-safe (>=4.5)** — `text/bg` 17.56, `text/surface` 16.21, `text/border` 14.28, `bg/accent` 6.90, `surface/accent` 6.37, `accent/border` 5.61, `primary/on-primary` 4.70, `bg/on-primary` 19.74, `surface/on-primary` 18.22, `border/on-primary` 16.05
- **UI-safe (>=3.0 and <4.5)** — `bg/primary` 4.20, `text/primary` 4.18, `surface/primary` 3.88, `primary/border` 3.42
- **Decorative (below 3.0)** — `text/accent` 2.54, `primary/accent` 1.64, `bg/border` 1.23, `bg/surface` 1.08. Hairlines and fills only, never the sole carrier of meaning.

**The trap this contract exists to catch:** `primary` (indigo) clears only 4.20 against `bg` — it is **UI-safe, not text-safe**. It may fill a chart bar or a border; it may never be body or label text on the background. Emphasised text and numerals use `accent` (6.90 against bg).

Contrast matrix result: 10 text-safe, 4 UI-safe, 7 decorative. No failing pairing shipped.

## Type

- Headings and numerals: **Unbounded** (bold geometric display, confident without tipping playful)
- Body: **Albert Sans**
- Numerals use tabular figures so columns align.

## Shape and density

Per the premium mood spec: flat or hairline-bordered, minimal shadow, 4 to 8px radius. Rules replace shadows. No rounded-full pills, no drop shadows used for depth.

Spacing scale (one set, reused): `4 8 12 16 24 32 48 64 96 128`.
Card padding at least 24px. Internal padding never exceeds the gap to the next element.

## Motion

Slide change plus a staggered content reveal. A scene change is allowed past 300ms because it is a scene change, not UI feedback. `prefers-reduced-motion` collapses everything to an instant swap. No `transition: all`, no `ease-in` on the reveal, no `scale(0)`, no layout-property animation.

## Assets

**None fetched, deliberately.** A strategy report's imagery is its data visualisation, which is drawn code-native as inline SVG at real scale: the concentration bar, the traffic breakdown, the retention bars with a 30% threshold line, the length buckets, the peer winner lengths, the demand comparison, and the peer benchmark table. Decorative photography would read as a blog post and would compete with the numbers. Icons are limited to the closing steps.

## Copy rules applied

The deck is shipped copy, so it follows Joel's writing rules on top of the anti-slop gate: no em dashes (a HIGH failure in the ruleset), no negative parallelism, no Rule of Three, no "Let's", no buzzwords, no hedges.
